LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At English Pathshala, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you submit an inquiry, book a demo session, enroll in a course, or contact us via WhatsApp or phone. This may include your full name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, learning goals, and course preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Coordinate free diagnostic demo sessions and academic assessments.
  • Connect you with senior language mentors via phone or WhatsApp.
  • Deliver customized course syllabi, batch schedules, and learning materials.
  • Provide student support, mock test scores, and feedback clinics.

3. WhatsApp & Direct Communication

When you initiate a WhatsApp conversation or request demo details through our website forms, you consent to receive direct educational advice and course updates. We respect your privacy and never sell, rent, or share your contact numbers with third-party marketers.

4. Data Security

We implement administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or to update your contact preferences, please reach out to our team at English Pathshala, Rajajipuram, Lucknow, or call/WhatsApp us directly at +91 70038 76568.
